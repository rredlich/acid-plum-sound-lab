Acid Plum Field Guide

Seven note keys, six parameter keys, a knob and a stick. Each step below shows which keys do what in that mode, and which lights tell you where you are.

STEP 0

Quick start: play chords and melodies

Switch it on and play. The Acid Plum starts in C major, chord mode. Each note key plays one chord of the key, so everything you press fits together.

  • NOTE 1–7Chords C · Dm · Em · F · G · Am · B°. The key lights while it sounds.
  • PAR4Chord ↔ melody. purple = chords, light blue = single notes.
  • KnobVolume. The knob light glows blue, brighter = louder.
  • Stick · chordsPush in 8 directions to change the chord: up = major ↔ minor, right = 7th, down = sus4, left = diminished, and more on the diagonals.
  • Stick · melodyUp / down = one octave higher / lower. Right = vibrato, left = tremolo.
STEP 1

Pick a synth sound

Press PAR1, then a note key. You hear a short C–E–G in the new sound, and that key stays purple so you know which one is active.

  • PAR1Open the sound page (PAR1 purple). Press again to leave.
  • NOTE 1–7Sine · Lo-fi keys · Chill pad · Techno stab · Chiptune · Strong · Rock/punk.

Design your own sounds in the Acid Plum Sound Lab and send them over USB. The bank key flashes green when one arrives. A recorded loop keeps the sound it was recorded with.

STEP 2

Choose a key and octave

Press PAR2, then a note key to move everything to another major key. The tonic chord plays as an arpeggio to confirm.

  • PAR2Open the key page (PAR2 purple). Press again to leave.
  • NOTE 1–7C · D · E · F · G · A · B major.
  • Stick ↑ / ↓One octave up or down per push. Let it return to the centre before the next push.

Loops you've already recorded keep their notes. Changing key only affects what you play next.

STEP 3

Add effects

Press PAR3. Each note key is one effect: tap it to switch it on or off, then turn the knob to set how much. The effects work on everything: what you play and your loops.

  • PAR3Open the effects page (PAR3 purple). Press again to leave; effects stay on and PAR3 stays dimly lit.
  • NOTE 1–7Echo · Chorus · Tone · Drive · Pump · Gate · Wobble.
  • KnobAmount of the last effect you tapped (Tone: darker ← → brighter; Gate: speed of the chop).

Effects that are on light purple; the one the knob edits is the brightest. Echo, Pump and Gate follow your tempo.

STEP 4

Set the tempo and loop length

Press PAR6. The metronome starts so you can feel the loop before recording it.

  • NOTE 1–7Loop length: 1 to 7 bars (default 4).
  • Stick ↑ / ↓Beats per bar, 2 to 12 (default 4).
  • KnobTempo, 40–240 BPM (default 120).

The selected bars light light blue; the bar you're in flashes purple on each beat. Once a loop is recorded, bars and beats are locked; tempo can still change.

STEP 5

Record loops

PAR5 is the orange key. It works from any page, so you can loop while choosing sounds or effects.

  • Tap PAR5One bar of count-in clicks, then it records for the whole loop and starts playing it back.
  • Tap againAdds a layer from the next bar 1 while the others play. Up to 4 layers. Tap while waiting to cancel.
  • Hold PAR5After 0.6 s it turns red. Tap it again to delete the newest layer (or wait 3 s to cancel).

PAR5 pulses purple on the beat while recording or waiting. Anything you play live over the loop is just for fun: it's only recorded while a recording is running.

Good to know

  • Keep your hands off the stick while switching on: it measures its centre then.
  • Hold PAR1 while switching on to bring back the 7 factory sounds (all sound keys flash green).
  • On any page, the active PAR key is purple. Press it again (or another PAR) to get back to playing.
